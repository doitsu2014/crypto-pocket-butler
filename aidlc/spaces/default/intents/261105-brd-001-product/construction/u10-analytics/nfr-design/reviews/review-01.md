### Summary

All three prior Majors are fixed: traceability now declares and covers 34 upstream requirements including NFR17.1 to NFR17.8 with real targets; the security design has Transport (TLS, encrypted overlay, `sslmode=require`) and Supply chain sections, and NFR9.1 and NFR12.1 point at them; and the gateway boundary is enforced by Analytics validating the JWT against Supabase JWKS with `X-Auth-Subject` matching the subject, the admin role taken from the validated token, and the erase caller resolved through `analytics.service_token_registry` with an Identity-only allow-list. The remaining items are small consistency edits (the flag bucket formula, the missing-salt statements, the erase executor, the aggregation lookback, the salt function's Vault grant and the C12 description), so the stage is READY.
