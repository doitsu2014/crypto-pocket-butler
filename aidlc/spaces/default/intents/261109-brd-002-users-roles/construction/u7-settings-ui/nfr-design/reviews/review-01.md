### Summary

The design is well grounded: it names the real helpers, routes, budgets and limits, records its cross-cutting gateway/contract dependencies honestly instead of assuming them, and its non-denying read mechanism is implementable on the existing stored-record lookup, including the `deleted` tombstone path. The one substantive gap is that NFR11.1 is marked covered by a per-route budget the gateway actually keys per bearer token rather than per user; the remaining items are documentation-level inconsistencies a developer would otherwise have to resolve by guessing.
