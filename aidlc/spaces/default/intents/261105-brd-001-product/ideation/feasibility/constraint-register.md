## Notes for synthesis

- **Launch-blocking:** CON-025 (market-data display/storage/derivation), CON-039 (exchange commercial-use/retention), CON-053 (licence register including RPC/indexer terms), CON-024 (budget closure), CON-021/CON-043 (pre-collection gate), CON-037/CON-042/CON-054 (data protection, retention and erasure), CON-038/CON-052 (consumer subscription and translated legal documents) and CON-014/CON-029 (availability). These are the rows that can stop launch without an engineering failure.
- **Unresolved tension:** CON-013 (1-minute collection, 5-minute publish) and CON-025 (storage/derivation rights) are compatible on cadence but only if the vendor grants storage and derivation. The earlier "24-hour cache ceiling" reading was wrong: "refresh at least every 24 h" is a maximum-staleness rule, not a minimum cache duration; the real conflict is the no-storage/no-derivation clause. Treat the combined position as unresolved.
- **Second-order cost:** CON-006 (no cross-schema grants) plus CON-008 (no message queues) push composition and deferred work into HTTP calls and Postgres-backed job records. This is an accepted architectural cost, not a defect, but it raises the value of the Asset Registry and Job Orchestration services.
- **Presentation and localisation are fixed, not open:** CON-002 fixes the entire presentation stack. The earlier note calling presentation "an open design decision" was wrong. **Localisation is a constraint, not an open design decision (CON-052)** — translated UI, notifications and legal documents are launch obligations where the market requires them. This supersedes the `work/constraint-register/fragment.md` "Not constraints" list, which wrongly excluded localisation scope; this canonical register is authoritative.
- **Gateway exceptions are enumerated:** CON-005 lists Supabase Auth and WalletConnect as browser-to-external exceptions, and the internal service token on scheduled job paths. The user-path JWT claim remains correct; the earlier "only Supabase Auth" wording was incomplete.
- **UK timing:** CON-021/CON-038 and assessment §4.2 split the **current** UK financial-promotion/arranging position from the **future** FCA authorisation regime (applications 30 September 2026; expected in force 25 October 2027). A 9–11 month MVP from November 2026 lands between August and October 2027 — just before or at commencement — so the current regime governs launch and the new regime is a narrow post-launch gate.
- **MiCA basis:** CON-022 and assessment §4.1 replace the Recital 83 wallet-software rationale with "supplies none of the Art 3(1)(16) services". The conclusion may survive; the cited exclusion does not fit a hosted aggregator.
- **Accessibility:** CON-023 records EN 301 549, the accessibility statement/feedback duty and the possible microenterprise exemption, rather than asserting WCAG 2.2 AA as the legal standard.
- **Chain coverage:** CON-019 maps "four families" to ten chains and names WalletConnect and CSV as committed Must scope, with xpub/watch-only as a Should fast-follow. The earlier "4 chain families" wording understated the build and the coverage claim.
- **Skill profile:** CON-020 and assessment §6 record that the team's Rust/Axum/sqlx, React and Swarm skill profile is assumed, not evidenced; the feasibility question set never asked it. Delivery planning must confirm it or budget training/hiring.
- **Benchmarks and FX:** CON-040 adds licensing rows separate from CON-025's market-data row; exchangerate.host's free tier is non-commercial and daily-tier history is insufficient for local-currency P&L at transaction timestamps.
- **Sanctions:** CON-050 records the conditional provider-screening dependency; NFR-LEG-07 is Should, but a provider term can make it a launch requirement.
- **Capacity numbers are planning assumptions:** CON-051 states the launch numbers explicitly so C10 has a pass/fail definition; replace them with observed data after the first month and re-run the load test.
- **Licence classes are consolidated:** CON-053 closes the fourth class (public RPC/indexer) that CON-025/CON-039/CON-040 did not cover, and makes the register evidence an activation gate rather than a documentation exercise.
- **Retention is a product rule, not a cleanup job:** CON-054 defines what survives erasure and why; the erasure drill and the backup-retention statement are launch evidence.
