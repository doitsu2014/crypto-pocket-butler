### Summary

Three findings are blocking, all of them cross-artifact rather than stylistic: the NFR22 "never in logs" claim does not reach the URL that carries the raw provider subject, the submit path has no client-side deadline so a hung gateway strands the screen with no recovery surface, and the idempotency key is discarded on remount while the documented recovery path and the C6 contract assume it still protects a repeated grant. The four minors are precision fixes a developer could otherwise carry as unstated assumptions.
