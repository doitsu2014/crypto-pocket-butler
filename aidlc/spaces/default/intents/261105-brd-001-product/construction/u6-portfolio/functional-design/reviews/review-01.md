### Summary

All four Criticals and the Major are fixed and verified in the artifacts and the shared contracts. C1 now defines every SPA-facing portfolio operation — create/list/get, account assign/move, target save with `?preview=true`, intent create with `?preview=true` and cancel, trade record, and a C1 snapshot history distinct from C8 — and C7 defines `POST /v1/users/{userId}/trades` with a client idempotency key and the ledger transaction id. The immediate allocation update now has a TradeOverlay applied to C5 holdings and removed on a matching sync, the post-trade recompute also evaluates guardrails, and the Guardrail entity stores `isOutside`, `lastCrossingKey` and `crossedAt` with the C9 payload. The earlier Minor items are reflected in the rules: unpriced lines carry quantity and status with an excluded count, FX provenance includes source and `derivedFrom`, the threshold is absolute, the allocation denominator excludes unpriced holdings, snapshots are hourly plus manual, and BR3.4 requires a target for every guardrail. The remaining items are Minor consistency and precision fixes — stale summary tables and workflow wording, the state machine still using signed drift, the BR3.4 traceability, the guardrail payload versus the U9 allow-list, the canonical-key reconciliation definition, the overlay's account attribution, the base-currency read and the manual-refresh/history query details.
