### Summary

The reporting service is well structured and its window, lease, retry, em-dash and privacy behaviour are sound, but the email path is broken at the C9 boundary: it submits `brief.ready` to a service that only accepts `brief`, so no brief email can be delivered. The C1 route paths and the brief body also diverge from the agreed contract, so the SPA surface and provenance/coverage requirements are not yet implementable as written.
