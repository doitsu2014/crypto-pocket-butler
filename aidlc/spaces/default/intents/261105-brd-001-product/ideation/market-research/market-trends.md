# Market Trends

Decision-oriented read for a read-only, non-custodial portfolio product launching globally by jurisdiction. Vendor estimates are labelled; unverifiable claims are marked **unverified**.

## Addressable Market (TAM/SAM/SOM, with explicit assumptions)

No credible category forecast exists: Grand View Research's page returned "information ... currently being developed" with no size, CAGR or methodology, so treat any forecast as unverified marketing ([Grand View Research](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/crypto-portfolio-tracker-market-report)). Sizing is bottom-up from owner counts and price anchors.

Owner counts diverge by vendor: a16z about 716 million in 2025, up 16% ([a16z](https://a16zfrontier.com/posts/article/state-of-crypto-report-2025/)); Crypto.com 741 million in December 2025 rising to 774 million by June 2026, +4.5% ([Crypto.com](https://crypto.com/en/research/crypto-market-sizing-report-h1-2026)); Triple-A "over 560 million" for 2024 with no disclosed method ([Triple-A](https://triple-a.io/crypto-ownership/)). Only Pew is an independent probability sample ([Pew](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2026/06/08/about-1-in-5-americans-have-used-crypto-republicans-use-has-ticked-up/)). Take 700–775 million as the ceiling.

| Layer | Estimate | Explicit assumptions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TAM | ~$0.4–3.35B/yr at the committed band (~$4.0B at the competitor anchor) | 700–775M owners × $60–170/yr × 1–3% paid penetration. The committed band is $60–144/yr ($5–12/mo); $150–170 is the competitor anchor ([CoinStats](https://coinstats.app/pricing), [Delta](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/delta-by-etoro/id1288676542)). |
| SAM | ~15–45M users | Pew's 19% "invested in or used" rate ([Pew](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2026/06/08/about-1-in-5-americans-have-used-crypto-republicans-use-has-ticked-up/)) applied to ~260M US adults (planning proxy, **unverified**) implies ~50M US users; assume Tier-1 markets hold 100–150M users and 15–30% use two or more venues. **Low confidence** — no public multi-venue survey exists and Pew measures lifetime use, not current ownership. |
| SOM | ~45k–270k payers; ~$2.7–39M ARR | 10–20% of SAM registers (1.5–9M), 3% convert at the committed $60–144/yr. $100M ARR needs ~694k–1.67M payers (~23–56M registrants): above the modeled SOM funnel, and reachable only if more than half of the 45M SAM ceiling registers at 3% conversion at the band top — so $100M requires higher ARPU, a larger SAM, or expansion. |

This is a large-user, thin-ARPU market. At the committed retail price, $100M ARR is unreachable within the modeled funnel; it requires higher ARPU, a larger SAM, or expansion into P&L and advisor segments ([CoinStats](https://coinstats.app/pricing)). The TAM applies paid penetration to all owners, while the SOM applies registration and conversion only to the multi-venue SAM — which is why the implied payer rate is lower (0.3–0.6% of SAM).

## Adoption and Demand Signals

Growth decelerated while usage held: owners grew +16% in the year to 2025 ([a16z](https://a16zfrontier.com/posts/article/state-of-crypto-report-2025/)) and 741M→774M (~9% annualized) in H1 2026 ([Crypto.com](https://crypto.com/en/research/crypto-market-sizing-report-h1-2026)), and market cap roughly halved in 2026 — yet transfers under $100 rose 78.4% and $100–$1,000 58.6% ([Chainalysis 2026](https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/2026-global-crypto-adoption-index/)). Demand persists through drawdowns.

The base is mostly passive: only 40–70 million active users against 716 million owners ([a16z](https://a16zfrontier.com/posts/article/state-of-crypto-report-2025/)). Activation is a product problem — the first valuation must land in minutes.

Geography shifts toward lower-ARPU growth markets: APAC on-chain activity grew 69% and Latin America 63%, against North America 49% and Europe 42% ([Chainalysis 2025](https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/2025-global-crypto-adoption-index/)). The 2026 top ten is Brazil, US, Nigeria, Japan, South Korea, India, Ukraine, Thailand, South Africa, Canada ([Chainalysis 2026](https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/2026-global-crypto-adoption-index/)); the US is institutional-heavy — 2nd in institutional value, 10th in retail centralized activity ([Chainalysis 2025](https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/2025-global-crypto-adoption-index/)).

Aggregators scale in wallets, not revenue: Zerion reports 10M+ wallets ([Zerion](https://zerion.io/)) and CoinStats 20,000+ coins and 120+ blockchains ([CoinStats](https://coinstats.app/)).

Trust binds: 63% of US adults lacked confidence in crypto's safety ([Pew 2024](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2024/10/24/majority-of-americans-arent-confident-in-the-safety-and-reliability-of-cryptocurrency/)), and reported fraud losses hit $9.3B in 2024, up 66% ([IC3](https://www.ic3.gov/AnnualReport/Reports/2024_IC3Report.pdf)).

## Regulatory and Compliance Shifts

**MiCA defines the product boundary.** Non-custodial wallet software is excluded ([Recital 83](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1114/oj/eng)), but personalised advice and discretionary portfolio management are licensed under Art 3(1)(16)(h)–(i), (24)–(25); execution and reception/transmission of orders are separately licensed under (e) and (g) respectively — relevant to user-initiated order intents ([MiCA](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1114/oj/eng)). Grandfathering ends 1 July 2026 ([ESMA](https://www.esma.europa.eu/esmas-activities/digital-finance-and-innovation/markets-crypto-assets-regulation-mica)), and review consultations closed 31 August 2026, so rules may move again ([EC](https://finance.ec.europa.eu/digital-finance/crypto-assets_en)).

Other gates: UK applications opened 30 September 2026, in force expected 25 October 2027 ([FCA](https://www.fca.org.uk/firms/new-regime-cryptoasset-regulation)); Singapore's DTSP regime generally will not license offshore-only providers ([MAS](https://www.mas.gov.sg/news/media-releases/2025/mas-clarifies-regulatory-regime-for-digital-token-service-providers)); US CLARITY passed the House 294–134 but Senate cloture failed 49–50 in September 2026 ([Orrick](https://www.orrick.com/en/Insights/2026/10/The-CLARITY-Act-Stalls-in-the-Senate-Whats-Next-for-Digital-Asset-Regulation)). The EBA's 2025 opinion addresses PSD2/MiCA interplay but does not establish a PSD2-style access right to exchange accounts ([EBA Op 2025/08](https://www.eba.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2025-06/e2958c99-a1b0-4b07-9d31-bcba0a28dbe7/Opinion%20on%20the%20interplay%20between%20PSD2%20and%20MiCA.pdf)); no such right appeared in the sources reviewed. Integration therefore assumes user-created read-only keys. [assumption]

Data obligations bind: Coinbase's market-data terms forbid redistribution, derived valuations and AI training, capping liability at $1,000 ([Coinbase](https://www.coinbase.com/legal/market_data)); CoinGecko requires attribution and 24-hour cache refresh ([CoinGecko](https://www.coingecko.com/en/api_terms)). Wallet addresses and keyed hashes can be personal data, requiring a DPIA ([EDPB](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/system/files/2026-07/edpb_guidelines_202502_blockchain_v2_en.pdf)). Accessibility became legally required for EU consumer services on 28 June 2025 ([EAA](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2019/882/oj/eng)) against WCAG 2.2 ([W3C](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/)).

## Implications for the MVP

1. **Lead with reconciliation, not chain breadth.** >99% accuracy needs transfer matching, missing-cost-basis detection and a visible reconciliation queue — the failures substitutes disclose ([CoinTracker](https://support.cointracker.com/hc/en-us/articles/4413071375505-Troubleshoot-portfolio-tracking-issues)). Launch coverage (5 venues and 4 chain families) starts below the 200–700-integration table-stakes bar; the roadmap closes it and the launch claim is reconciliation quality, not breadth. DeFi/staking/LP position decoding remains post-MVP.
2. **Price at $5–12/mo with a free first valuation**, at or under Delta/CoinStats, capping transactions or assets rather than features ([CoinStats](https://coinstats.app/pricing), [Delta](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/delta-by-etoro/id1288676542)).
3. **Keep decision support generic.** Personalised recommendations or discretionary management trigger MiCA CASP authorisation; order intents must be a documented handoff to a licensed third party ([MiCA](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1114/oj/eng)).
4. **Buy data, do not build it.** CoinGecko Analyst at $129/mo meets the 5-minute target; CoinMarketCap's $79 Startup tier is a second source, both behind a port ([CoinGecko](https://www.coingecko.com/en/api/pricing), [CoinMarketCap](https://coinmarketcap.com/api/pricing/)).
5. **Sequence jurisdictions.** Tier 1: US, EU (MiCA-passportable), UK, Japan, South Korea, Canada, Australia, Singapore. Tier 2: Brazil, India, Nigeria, Indonesia, Vietnam, Philippines, Thailand, Turkey, Argentina, South Africa, Ukraine — local currency and language as the wedge ([Chainalysis 2026](https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/2026-global-crypto-adoption-index/)). Tier 1 is chosen for regulatory and monetization readiness with a MiCA-passportable EU entry, not for adoption rank; the fastest-growing adoption markets are the Tier 2 wedge, where local pricing and language carry the differentiation. Singapore is Tier 1 only with a locally licensed partner (the DTSP regime generally excludes offshore-only providers), and the UK is sequenced for pre-positioning ahead of its expected 2027 regime. The tiering is a planning recommendation, not a ranking; Ukraine appears in the 2026 top ten by adoption but is sequenced later pending legal review. [assumption]
6. **Ship PWA-first.** Declarative Web Push works from iOS 18.4, but iOS install is manual and beforeinstallprompt is unavailable, so activation cannot rely on install or push ([WebKit](https://webkit.org/blog/16535/meet-declarative-web-push/), [MDN](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/Progressive_web_apps/Guides/Making_PWAs_installable)); dashboards must meet INP at p75 ([web.dev](https://web.dev/blog/inp-cwv-march-12)).
7. **Use tax reporting as a hook, not the product:** US 1099-DA, EU DAC8 and OECD CARF exchanges create cost-basis demand without CPB filing taxes ([IRS](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1099da), [EC DAC8](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/taxation/tax-transparency-cooperation/administrative-co-operation-and-mutual-assistance/directive-administrative-cooperation-dac/dac8_en), [OECD](https://www.oecd.org/en/about/news/announcements/2024/10/crypto-asset-reporting-framework-and-amended-common-reporting-standard-oecd-releases-it-format-for-transmitting-information-and-issues-interpretative-guidance.html)).

## Assumptions & Open Questions

Assumptions: TAM uses 1–3% paid penetration across the $60–170/yr range; SOM uses the committed $60–144/yr band; SAM's 15–30% multi-venue incidence is assumed, not measured; SOM assumes 10–20% of SAM registers.

Open questions: how many retail owners use two or more venues; willingness to pay at local pricing in Brazil, India, Nigeria, Indonesia, Vietnam and the Philippines; where authorities draw the MiCA line between generic decision support and personalised advice; whether an order-intent handoff is reception/transmission of orders and who holds the licence; which vendor grants worldwide redistribution and derived-valuation rights at retail price. CoinStats' Degen tier is listed at $62.91/month billed annually when the page renders but is not visible to a plain fetch, so treat it as region/render dependent. Koinly Trader is ~$199 per tax year in the cited 2025 review; current official pricing is **unverified**. Zerion Premium is $99/yr per wallet as published.
