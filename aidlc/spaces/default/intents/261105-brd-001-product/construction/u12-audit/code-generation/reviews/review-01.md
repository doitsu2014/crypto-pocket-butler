### Summary

The audit design is well structured and the append/idempotency, event-class derivation, secret rejection on target/context, support expiry-as-deny, and retention drop-versus-batch decision are implemented as specified, with a clean Clean Architecture split and a real test suite. However, the retention role cannot operate on the partitions it is asked to read and drop, the seed-phrase detector misses ordinary BIP-39 phrases, and any registered service can trigger a destructive privacy deletion, so the service would ship with broken retention and weakened audit guarantees.
