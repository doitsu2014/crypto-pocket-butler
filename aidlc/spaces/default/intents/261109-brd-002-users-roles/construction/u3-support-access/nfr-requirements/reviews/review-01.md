### Summary

The security artifact itself is the strongest part of the stage — the allowlist, the uniform refusal, the fail-closed audit ordering and the no-callback rule are concrete and implementable. What blocks approval is arithmetic elsewhere: the read path's latency and availability targets cannot hold together (a timeout set at the source's own p99, two fail-closed dependencies plus an audit append, against an inherited 99.9%), the outbox figures describe a drainer other than the shipped one, and the migration's down step is designed to refuse on rows the design also says never age out.
