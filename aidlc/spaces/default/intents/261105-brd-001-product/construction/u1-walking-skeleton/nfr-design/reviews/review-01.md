### Summary

The design is well structured but not implementable as written: audit is specified as fire-and-forget while the registry rule requires refusing any change that cannot be audited, and the timeout and retry numbers cannot coexist. Below that, rate limiting has no shared state across four replicas, principal resolution has neither a cache policy nor a single role source, and the observability requirements are entirely missing from traceability.
