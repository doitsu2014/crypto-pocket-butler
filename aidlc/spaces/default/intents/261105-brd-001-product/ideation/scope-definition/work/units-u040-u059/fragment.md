# Intent Backlog Fragment — U-040 to U-059

**Intent:** `261105-brd-001-product` · **Stage:** `ideation/scope-definition` · **Status:** fragment for synthesis (not canonical)
**Owns:** API Gateway, Job Orchestration, Audit, the SPA, and cross-cutting launch obligations.
**Unit range:** U-040 to U-059 only. Twenty proto-Units, one capability each.

**Basis:** confirmed Q6 (Option B, ten launch services, 5 engineers, 9-month target with a written cut list, 10–11 months for full committed scope), Q7 (EU MiCA and UK first, legal capped at ~USD 50,000 fixed fee, UK live only after counsel sign-off), Q8 (Notification, Analytics and Support Console deferred to post-MVP with documented degraded paths and an explicit BRD deviation), Q1 (full Option B retail slice), Q2 (risk-first with a walking skeleton), Q3 (no external hard date). Constraints from the feasibility assessment and constraint register CON-001…CON-054 are binding.

**Fixed constraints reflected in every unit:** Rust/Axum/sqlx Clean Architecture workspaces; React 18/TS 5 SPA with Yarn ≥ 6; Supabase Postgres/Auth; one schema and role per service with no cross-schema grants (the Vault platform-schema exception is the only recorded exception); no RLS; services read each other only over HTTP; no message queues; Docker Swarm; secrets in Supabase Vault. Read-only exchange credentials, no custody, no automatic trading, no fiat ramp, no tax filing or advice. Launch coverage: five exchanges, ten chains, WalletConnect and CSV committed, xpub/watch-only Should fast-follow. Launch gates C1–C16 apply as written in the scope document.

**Conventions used in this fragment**

- `BRD requirement IDs` names the requirements that justify the capability; the coverage ledger in section 3 carries the complete, exhaustive Must mapping for the ten BRDs in scope (BRD-002, 003, 010, 011, 012, 014, 017, 018, 019, 021).
- Requirements owned by a service capability whose units are outside U-040–U-059 are recorded in the ledger by owning service ("Identity units", "Account units", "Asset Registry units", "Market Data units", "Portfolio units", "Ledger units", "Reporting units", "Billing units"). Synthesis must bind those names to their actual unit IDs; they are not orphans.
- WSJF is shown as `CoD / Size = score`, where cost of delay and job size are each scored 1–10 (higher cost of delay = more urgent; higher job size = more effort). The one-line rationale follows the score.
- Effort is engineer-weeks for a 5-engineer team at the feasibility assessment's 80% effective capacity, and includes tests, contract tests and accessibility work for the unit — not audit, legal or licence fees, which are non-salary budget lines.
- Release values: `MVP`, `deferred` (full capability post-MVP; a degraded path ships at launch), `post-MVP`. No unit in this fragment is post-MVP without a launch-time degraded path.

## 2. Sequencing (risk-first, per Q2)

The units are ordered for execution as risk-first slices, not by ID:

1. **M0 walking skeleton:** U-040, U-043, U-046, U-050, U-059, then U-047 against vendor sandboxes with synthetic fixtures. These prove the gateway trust boundary, the scheduler and its dead-man path, the audit insert-only role and the availability evidence before real data exists.
2. **M1 gates and first real user:** U-051 before any real user; U-042 and U-041 as the gateway hardens; U-045 on the address path; U-048 for read-only sync; U-049 snapshots without billing.
3. **M2–M3 differentiator and retention:** U-044 full workspace, U-049 briefs and billing scheduling, U-052 privacy, U-053 locale foundation.
4. **M4–M5 commercial and hardening:** U-054 accessibility remediation, U-055 PWA, U-056/U-057/U-058 degraded paths, U-059 drill and status page, pen-test remediation.

The ordering is a preference, not a dependency override: U-051 must precede the first real user regardless of slice position, and U-059's external monitor must be green for seven days before public beta.

## 6. Notes for the synthesising agent

1. **Do not renumber.** These are proto-Units U-040–U-059; synthesis may merge or split only with an explicit traceability note, and must not reuse the range for other capabilities.
2. **Bind service-unit names.** The ledger names Identity, Account, Asset Registry, Market Data, Portfolio, Ledger, Reporting and Billing units that are outside this fragment. Replace each with the actual unit ID from the sibling fragments and confirm no Must is left unbound.
3. **Effort reconciliation.** This fragment totals 74 engineer-weeks. The whole committed scope is 108–186 engineer-weeks; if the other fragments push the sum above the 5 × 9-month ceiling of ~36 effective engineer-months, apply the cut list in this order: U-055 installability, extra locales beyond the fixed set, optional alert types, then deferred-path polish. Never cut U-051, U-050, U-052 or the C-gate evidence.
4. **Two deliberate deviations must survive synthesis.** The Q8 service deferrals and the partial FR-L10N-15 email localisation. If either is dropped, the backlog falsely claims complete Must coverage.
5. **One scope caveat.** The confirmed constraints commit WalletConnect and CSV and keep xpub/watch-only as a Should fast-follow. The scope document also records PWA installability as Should. Both are reflected here; do not upgrade them to Must without a scope change at the approval gate.
6. **Terminology.** "Order intent" is a recorded, human-approved, never-transmitted handoff. No unit in this fragment may create an execution or transmission capability, and no acceptance criterion may require a venue order call.
