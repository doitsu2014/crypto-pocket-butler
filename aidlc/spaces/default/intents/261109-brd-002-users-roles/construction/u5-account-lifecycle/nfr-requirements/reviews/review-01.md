### Summary

The NFR set is mostly measurable and the outbox and cascade reasoning is sound, but NFR22.1 describes a tombstone keyed on a column that does not exist (the real subject hash is deleted with `user_personal`), and several targets are internally inconsistent — the sweep's per-account and batch budgets, the retry bound's missing claim predicate and terminal state, an unowned read-only rule, and traceability that maps NFR24 to atomicity instead of freshness. Those are fixable in this stage, and the review fails on the tombstone claim plus the major target gaps.
