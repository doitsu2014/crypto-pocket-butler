### Summary

The unit's central recovery journey rests on a false premise: `pending_deletion`, `disabled` and `deleted` are already refused with 403 by the identity principal resolution, so status-keyed entry routing and the new owner-scoped deletion reads cannot work as written, and the spec's account of today's behaviour contradicts both the code and u5's contract. Fixing that premise, the misnamed entry-routing endpoint, the missing gateway entries for the export flow, and the conflicting GrantBanner contract would make the design implementable; the remaining items are copy-level corrections.
