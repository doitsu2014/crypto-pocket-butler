### Summary

The BRD coverage is genuinely strong and the interview answers are applied consistently, but the headline security promise (revocation ends a session on the next request) is weakened to a documented residual window without an explicit signed-off deviation, the 30-day cap has no measurable anchor, and the signup-gate token is an undefined new mechanism; those need product decisions before engineering starts.
