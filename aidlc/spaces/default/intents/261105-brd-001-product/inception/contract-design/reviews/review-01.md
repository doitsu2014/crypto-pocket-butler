### Summary

The whole set of contracts is structurally valid YAML with no dangling references, and the interview answers (OpenAPI 3.1, `/v1`, RFC 7807, internal-only gateway) are followed. The blocking concern is coverage: the gateway contract the SPA must build against has four operations, two units (Reporting, Job Orchestration) never appear as providers, two Identity edges in the unit DAG have no contract, and the error, timeout, retry, idempotency and authentication rules are stated in prose without the values or per-operation declarations an implementer needs.
