### Summary

The design stays correctly inside the team's Docker Swarm plus Supabase, production-only deployment, and is mostly implementable, but it does not yet hold together on capacity, the worker, Vault, or recovery: the 60-connection budget is spent before the worker exists, nothing can wake the worker within the promised 2 s, the shredding key is described in a way the service cannot use, and a restore would resurrect a deleted user's key. The traceability file also drops the six NFRs that the monitoring design and the scan stage implement, so the coverage claim overstates the design.
