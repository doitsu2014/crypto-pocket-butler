### Summary

The stories are well-structured, fully traceable to FR14–FR20 and NFR21–NFR26, and consistently security-first; the two Majors are worth fixing before design rather than after. They are that the support role's read-only boundary is never exercised by an acceptance criterion, and that timezone, notification preferences, and search appear in story text without any criterion that tests them.
