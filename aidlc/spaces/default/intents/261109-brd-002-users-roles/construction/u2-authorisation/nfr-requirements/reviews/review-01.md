### Summary

The measurable targets are present and mostly well grounded, but the unit's central latency claim cannot hold: the C4 endpoint's 35 ms budget omits the audit write it must perform before returning a denial, and the status lookup is simultaneously specified as a local indexed read and as an HTTP call under the 5 s/10 s/three-attempt contract, with the 30-second cache described as both outage-only and normal-path. Four Major findings put this at NOT-READY; each is a decision an implementer would otherwise have to invent.
