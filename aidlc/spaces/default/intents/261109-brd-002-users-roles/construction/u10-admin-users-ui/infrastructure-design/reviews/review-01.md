### Summary

The unit is correctly scoped as a UI change (no new container, secret, migration or stack change) and nearly every platform fact in the specification matches the shipped stack and nginx/Dockerfiles. Three material accuracy problems block a straight READY: the gateway route accounting contradicts D-S1 by leaving the address-carrying `GET /v1/admin/users` "unchanged" and by presenting the already-declared `PUT …/role` entry as a new one whose 20/min budget would be silently shadowed; and the monitoring design attributes per-route and latency metrics to the gateway and Identity that their `/metrics` surfaces do not expose, so the latency SLIs (and the NFR2.1/NFR2.3 OK rows) currently rest on a surface that does not exist.
