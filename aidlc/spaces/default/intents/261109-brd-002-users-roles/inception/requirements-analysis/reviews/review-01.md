### Summary

The set is well grounded in the interview answers and the code knowledge base, the four statuses, 30-day recovery, BRD-017 dependency, `app_metadata.role`, the 60-minute support grant, the support metadata boundary, jurisdiction gating, self-declared age and the entitlements narrowing are all reflected consistently, and numbering (FR14–FR20, NFR21–NFR26) continues the BRD-001 record without collision, with `traceability.json` matching `requirements.md` exactly. It is not ready because four things a developer still has to guess: nothing maps a requirement back to a BRD-002 id, the support-grant requester is undefined and its acceptance criterion contradicts the implemented model, the `pending` status is left outside the four-status rule while the schema still provisions it, and NFR24 promises a role-change timing the token-based role resolution cannot deliver.
