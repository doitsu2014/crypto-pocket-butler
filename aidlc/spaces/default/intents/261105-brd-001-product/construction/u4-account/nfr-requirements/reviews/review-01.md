### Summary

The NFR set is substantive and mostly well grounded, but three defects make it unsafe to implement from: the two authentication/authorization requirements describe only the user-JWT path and would reject the service-token reads that C5, C11 and BR8.1 require; the derived ID `NFR9.1` means two unrelated things and the CSV import target has no inception NFR behind it; and a data-integrity requirement is filed under the dashboard-performance NFR while NFR8, which it actually serves, is declared not applicable. Fixing those three plus the unimplementable "key material never enters the service" target would clear the way.
