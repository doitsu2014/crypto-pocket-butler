### Summary

The platform choices are right and consistent with the walking skeleton on production-only Swarm, partitioning, and the append-only role split, but the design cannot be built as written: the boundary between `audit-api` and `audit-retention` is undesigned, the production migration and rollback path is unwired and names a role with no DDL rights, and the two-image split and several mandated CI gates are asserted rather than delivered. Two Critical and eight Major findings mean NOT-READY.
