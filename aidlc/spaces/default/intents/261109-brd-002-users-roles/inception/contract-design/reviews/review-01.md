### Summary

The security intent is present — the support metadata projection and the role and status decision shapes are recognisable — but the contract cannot be built as written: the no-disclosure rule is undermined by a distinguishable 403/404, several write operations have no request shape, the `/v1` surface is missing the routes the four UI units need, and a fifth `pending` status contradicts the approved four-status requirement. The C4 authorisation boundary is internally contradictory (HTTP contract versus an open question about a shared library) and leaves the role source unverified.
