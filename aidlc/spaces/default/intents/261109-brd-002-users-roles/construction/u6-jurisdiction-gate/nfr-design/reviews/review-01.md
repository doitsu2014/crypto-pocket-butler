### Summary

The design is well structured in most places, but the IP trust chain that the whole unit exists to enforce is not defined against the platform it runs on (CDN edge, first-entry X-Forwarded-For at the gateway), which lets a restricted user present an allowed address, and the latency budget and provider quota do not add up to the throughput and p95 targets the same unit claims. A further six gaps and five smaller inconsistencies are listed above for the approval gate to weigh.
