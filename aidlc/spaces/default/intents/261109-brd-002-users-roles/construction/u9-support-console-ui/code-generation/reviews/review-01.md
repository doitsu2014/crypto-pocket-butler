| R-01 | Major | support-console/src/screens/SupportShell.tsx > onCheckStatus handlers (lines 174-175 and 208) and the grant adoption effect (lines 81-85) | Both check-status controls are dead. ### Summary

S2's server half is solid and W1/W3/W5/W7 and the S2 gateway/route/migration work hold up against the design, but the console's W4 recovery loop is broken: both check-status controls are wired to a section change and never re-read `mine`, so a pending grant cannot become Active without a reload. Three smaller hard requirements are also unmet (S1 has no 15 s deadline, the metadata read refetches on window focus, and the TDD record claims failing output that is not in any artifact); all four are small, targeted fixes, not redesigns.
