# Intent Backlog — Fragment: Portfolio, Ledger, Reporting & Billing

**Producer:** aidlc-product-agent (proto-Units U-020–U-039)
**Intent:** `261105-brd-001-product` · **Stage:** `ideation/scope-definition` · **Status:** fragment for synthesis into `intent-backlog.md`
**Basis:** BRD-006, BRD-007, BRD-008, BRD-009, BRD-013, BRD-016, BRD-020; confirmed answers Q1–Q3 and feasibility decisions Q6–Q8; `feasibility-assessment.md` (§2.1, §2.4, §5) and `constraint-register.md` (CON-004…CON-013, CON-019, CON-025/026/039/040/051/053/054); `docs/architecture/README.md` §5 and §11; `docs/brds/DECISIONS.md` (D-003…D-007, D-009, D-010, D-013, D-016, D-018, D-020).

**Scope of this fragment.** The four launch services that carry the product's core loop: **Portfolio** (portfolio model, valuation, allocation, snapshots, orders, performance), **Ledger** (transactions, cost basis, P&L, reconciliation), **Reporting** (briefs), and **Billing** (plans, entitlements, subscriptions). Twenty capabilities, U-020 through U-039, sized against the Option B retail slice confirmed at Scope Q1 and sequenced risk-first per Q2. Other services and the shared SPA shell are costed in sibling fragments; this fragment assumes them as dependencies and does not re-cost them.

**Confirmed decisions honoured (not re-opened):** 10 launch services, 5 engineers, 9-month target with a written cut list and 10–11 months for full committed scope (feasibility Q6); EU MiCA plus UK first with UK live only after counsel sign-off (Q7); Notification, Analytics and Support Console deferred with documented degraded paths (Q8); full Option B retail slice in the MVP (Scope Q1); risk-first walking skeleton (Scope Q2); no external hard date (Scope Q3).

**Result in one line.** All **109 Must functional requirements** across the seven BRDs map to an MVP unit — **zero Must deferrals** in these domains. The fragment totals **47.5 engineer-weeks (≈11 engineer-months)**, about 30% of the 36 effective engineer-months available to a 5-engineer team over 9 months and consistent with the 25–43 engineer-month committed demand. A **5.0 engineer-week written cut list** inside these units brings it to 42.5 if the schedule tightens.

## 8. Decisions taken in this fragment (to be confirmed at synthesis)

These resolve proto-level ambiguities without contradicting a confirmed decision. Each is testable and reversible.

1. **Shared-account P&L is counted once at user level.** A shared account contributes to every portfolio that contains it, but a user-level P&L total counts each account once; portfolio-level views show the account's contribution and label the overlap. Otherwise two portfolios containing one account would double the user's apparent gain. (U-033)
2. **Cost basis scope is account plus asset, with a per-user method.** FIFO is the default and average cost is selectable per user (D-007); changing the method recomputes the whole user ledger and is audited. Portfolio P&L is a roll-up, not a second basis. (U-032, U-033)
3. **Recording a trade does not create a snapshot.** BRD-008 OQ-002 is answered for the MVP as "no": the trade updates the current valuation input immediately, and snapshots remain manual or scheduled with their own immutability rule. This keeps snapshots comparable and avoids snapshot spam. (U-027, U-034)
4. **Entitlement cache TTL is 60 seconds, with a synchronous Billing check on gated writes.** Reads may use the cached entitlement; creating an account, portfolio, export or API call checks Billing synchronously so a downgrade cannot be bypassed by a warm cache. Billing unavailability fails open for read-only portfolio access and closed for gated writes. (U-038)
5. **EOD snapshots are scheduled in per-user timezone buckets with single-flight per portfolio, date and type.** Empty portfolios are skipped by default (D-006); a missed bucket is detectable within 5 minutes by Job Orchestration. (U-027)
6. **Trade reconciliation flags `mismatched` at the first sync after 24 hours without a match, configurable.** The window is a configuration value so Operations can tune it once BRD-008 OQ-005 closes; the flag is always visible and never deletes the trade. (U-034)
7. **Past-due grace period is configuration, set by Finance before C9.** The mechanism (state, notice, downgrade after grace, data preserved) is built; the number is not invented here. (U-039)
