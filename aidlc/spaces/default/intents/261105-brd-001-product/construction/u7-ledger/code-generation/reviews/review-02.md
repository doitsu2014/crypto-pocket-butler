### Summary

All four prior Majors are fixed: the P&L now takes current prices and FX from C6 with unpriced marks that are never zero, coverage is built from Account's C5 holdings with every no-history or unpriced holding named in the gap, an unmatched external transfer_out consumes the outgoing lots with no realised gain while a matched transfer stays a no-op, and the totals carry a base currency with `currency`, `fxAsOf` and `fxEstimated` under the recorded C7 amendment. The Minors are in as well: the snapshot short-circuit, the inbound transfer's pending pair, rejection of a fee without a price, required TLS, strengthened traceability targets, and the OTLP and retention deferrals with their delivering stage. One new Major blocks: the Ledger now calls C6 but is not a C6 consumer or on Market Data's caller allow-list, and the domain design does not record the dependency, so the P&L would be refused at runtime until that contract change is recorded. The remaining items are the C6-outage behaviour, the read-path C6 latency budget, and the unexecuted adapter and coverage runs.
