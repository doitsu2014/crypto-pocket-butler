### Summary

The transaction design, tombstone move, edge CHECK and outbox are solid, but the boundaries around them are not closed: the deletion surface bypasses the status denial for disabled/deleted accounts, the internal `POST` ignores its path user, an admin restore strands the open request, the `pending → active` promotion escapes the transition log, the sweep runs one request per minute against a batch-of-100 design, and C7 is unreachable through the gateway for its two UI consumers.
