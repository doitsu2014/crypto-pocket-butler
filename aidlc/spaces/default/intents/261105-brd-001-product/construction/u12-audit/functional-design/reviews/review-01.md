### Summary

The design is well structured and its rules are mostly testable, but it assigns the audit unit work it cannot do under the project's data-boundary rules and leaves the support-access and operator APIs outside any shared contract. Three Critical findings block progression: cross-schema financial-record deletion, a support-session lifecycle with no inbound contract and two owners, and an audit service specified to serve reads it does not own.
