01 · Foundations

Principles

Three rules settle most design arguments before they start.

01

Say the number, then the context.

A value is never naked. It carries an as-of time and a source state, so the user can judge it instead of trusting it.

02

Quiet chrome, loud data.

Lacquer-dark surfaces, hairline borders, no decorative glow. Hierarchy comes from type scale and space, not ornament.

03

Color confirms, words decide.

Status always includes a label. Green, amber, and red never carry meaning alone, and the accent is never decoration.

07 · Practice

Do & don't

The short version, for the moment before an artifact ships.

Do

  • Show the as-of time and source state next to every value.
  • Use mono and right alignment for figures, and sign every change.
  • Label sample data and unpriced assets plainly.
  • Keep one primary action per view and one accent detail per screen.

Don't

  • Use green or red without a word, a sign, and a shape.
  • Add shadows to cards, inputs, or buttons.
  • Use the accent for decoration, gradients, or glow.
  • Invent balances, percentages, or metrics — label samples instead.

08 · Practice

Brand mark

A gilded seal: the PB monogram engraved in the system’s gold, with the Gilded Ledger wordmark. Use it as shipped — never redrawn, recolored, or rotated.

The lockup ships as logo.svg; the emblem alone is logo-mark.svg and doubles as the favicon. Gold on lacquer only — never on gold or ivory. Clear space around the emblem equals the ring’s radius, and the monogram never renders below 24px.
