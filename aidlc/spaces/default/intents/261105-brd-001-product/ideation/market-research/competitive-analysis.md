# Competitive Analysis

## Market Context

Retail crypto ownership is large but thin in engagement. Global owners reached roughly 774 million in H1 2026, up only 4.5% from 741 million in December 2025 ([Crypto.com](https://crypto.com/en/research/crypto-market-sizing-report-h1-2026)), while a16z counted about 716 million owners in 2025 and only 40–70 million active users ([a16z](https://a16zfrontier.com/posts/article/state-of-crypto-report-2025/)). Retail transfer activity grew in 2026 even as market cap roughly halved ([Chainalysis](https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/2026-global-crypto-adoption-index/)). Price anchors sit low: $4.99/month for Coinbase One Basic and Kraken+ ([Coinbase](https://www.coinbase.com/one), [Kraken](https://www.kraken.com/kraken-plus)), $13.99/month for CoinStats Premium ([CoinStats](https://coinstats.app/pricing)), and $99/year for Zerion Premium ([Zerion](https://zerion.io/premium)).

Two structural facts shape the opportunity. Venue fragmentation is real: incumbents claim 200–700+ integrations and 66–120+ chains ([Delta](https://delta.app/en/pro), [Koinly](https://koinly.io/), [CoinStats](https://coinstats.app/)). And trust is the binding constraint: 63% of US adults reported little or no confidence in crypto's safety and reliability, while crypto fraud losses reached $9.3B in 2024 ([Pew](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2024/10/24/majority-of-americans-arent-confident-in-the-safety-and-reliability-of-cryptocurrency/), [FBI IC3](https://www.ic3.gov/AnnualReport/Reports/2024_IC3Report.pdf)).

## Execution and Order-Intent Landscape

Three execution postures exist, and none matches the target position. Exchanges execute natively and own the order flow ([Coinbase](https://www.coinbase.com/one), [Binance](https://www.binance.com/en/fee/schedule)). Tax and tracking tools are read-only with no execution ([CoinTracker support](https://support.cointracker.com/hc/en-us/articles/4413071375505-Troubleshoot-portfolio-tracking-issues), [CoinLedger](https://coinledger.io/pricing)). Self-custodial wallets such as Zerion execute swaps inside the wallet, which requires the user to hold keys and assets at that venue ([Zerion](https://zerion.io/)). [assumption]

Crypto Pocket Butler's order intent is a deliberate fourth posture: the user records a buy or sell intent against a cross-venue view, and the product never places the order. That keeps custody and execution risk out of the product, but order transmission is a licensed activity under MiCA ([MiCA](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1114/oj/eng)). The intent must therefore be a documented handoff to a licensed venue rather than a routed order, and the competitive claim is cross-venue truth plus a recorded intent — not faster execution.

## Strengths to Match

Coverage is table stakes: 200–700+ integrations and 66–120+ chains ([Delta](https://delta.app/en/pro), [CoinStats home](https://coinstats.app/)). Read-only access is already the norm — CoinTracker advertises "Read-only connections" ([CoinTracker](https://www.cointracker.com/)), CoinLedger "read-only API" ([CoinLedger](https://coinledger.io/pricing)), CoinStats "read-only access only" ([CoinStats](https://coinstats.app/pricing/)). A generous free entry tier is expected: CoinStats gives 10 portfolios and 20,000 transactions free, Delta unlimited connections ([CoinStats](https://coinstats.app/pricing/), [Delta](https://delta.app/en/pro)).

## Weaknesses to Exploit

Reconciliation is the category's shared failure mode. CoinTracker states errors "are often caused by incomplete or incorrect transaction history" and that "a broken connection is the most common cause of a wrong number" ([CoinTracker support](https://support.cointracker.com/hc/en-us/articles/4413071375505-Troubleshoot-portfolio-tracking-issues)). CoinStats' reply on Trustpilot concedes it "does not support the full transaction history from Earn," producing synthetic fills and wrong P&L ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/coinstats.app)). Koinly users report CSV/CEX import friction ([Koinly review](https://cryptoadventure.com/koinly-review-2025-pricing-plans-and-features/)).

Single-venue blindness is the second gap: Coinbase, Binance and Kraken expose only their own balances and ship separate on-chain wallets ([Coinbase Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.coinbase.android), [Kraken Wallet](https://www.kraken.com/wallet), [Binance Web3](https://www.binance.com/en/web3wallet)). Coinbase's total P&L is defined as the sum of unrealized P/L "for all positions in your portfolio" ([Coinbase Help](https://help.coinbase.com/en/global-exchange/portfolio-balances/how-is-total-profit-and-loss-determined)). Third, P&L is gated: Delta locks gains reporting to PRO+ ([Delta](https://delta.app/en/pro)) and Zerion sells wallet P&L at $99/year per wallet ([Zerion](https://zerion.io/premium)).

## Differentiation Strategy

Win on the total position, plainly explained, at retail price. Three commitments:

1. **Cross-venue truth as the product, not a feature.** A visible reconciliation workflow — transfer matching, missing-cost-basis detection, stale-data badges — directly attacks the failure mode above. This is what makes the >99% valuation-accuracy metric credible rather than asserted.
2. **Local-currency, plain-language P&L.** Cost basis stays jurisdiction-agnostic in the domain model; the US per-wallet shift from 2025 ([CoinTracker](https://support.cointracker.com/hc/en-us/articles/4413071343889-Cost-basis-tracking-for-US-customers)) shows hard-coding one method is a trap. Prioritise markets where retail activity is growing fastest — APAC +69%, LatAm +63% ([Chainalysis](https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/2025-global-crypto-adoption-index/)).
3. **Trust as explicit product surface.** Read-only, no keys, no custody, no trading, stated on every connection screen — the posture Rotki validates as demand but confines to a desktop ([Rotki](https://rotki.com/)). Price in the $5–$12/month band, below Kubera and at or under Delta and CoinStats, with the first valuation free.

## Threats and Fast Followers

Incumbent bundling is the main risk: Delta now ships as "Delta by eToro" ([Delta listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/delta-by-etoro/id1288676542)), and Coinbase, Kraken and Binance each already surface a portfolio view plus a wallet. Exchanges have little incentive to build cross-venue reconciliation because they monetize fees and fee-waiver subscriptions, not tracking ([Coinbase](https://www.coinbase.com/one), [Kraken](https://www.kraken.com/kraken-plus)) — the gap should persist, but one bundling move compresses it. Storefront listing counts are mutable and were not retained as a stable citation; treat any such count as a point-in-time proxy for installs, not a user measure. Regulatory drag cuts both ways: MiCA grandfathering ends 1 July 2026 ([ESMA](https://www.esma.europa.eu/esmas-activities/digital-finance-and-innovation/markets-crypto-assets-regulation-mica)) and order transmission triggers CASP authorisation ([MiCA](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1114/oj/eng)), so a licensed competitor can reach order intents faster than we can.

## Assumptions & Open Questions

- No public benchmark exists for category churn or free-to-paid conversion, so the >=3% conversion and >=25% D30 targets cannot be validated against incumbents.
- Multi-venue incidence — the core driver of aggregation demand — has no public survey; a planning SAM of 15–45 million users is an inference, not a measured figure.
- Koinly's Trader plan is $199 per tax year in the cited 2025 review; the official page renders prices dynamically, so the current figure is **unverified**.
- Zerion Premium is $99/yr per wallet as published; its fiat billing terms are **unverified** ([Zerion](https://zerion.io/premium)). CoinStats' Degen tier is listed at $62.91/month billed annually when the page renders but is not visible to a plain fetch, so treat it as region/render dependent ([CoinStats](https://coinstats.app/pricing/)).
- Whether Coinbase's portfolio view includes cost basis for assets transferred in from other venues is undocumented and materially affects the size of the gap.
- Kubera's crypto reconciliation depth and cost-basis accuracy are **unverified**; the site publishes no methodology and states no minimum balance, so the high-net-worth segment is a positioning inference, not a measured cutoff.
- Competitors' order-execution capabilities were assessed from public marketing and pricing pages. In-wallet swap mechanics and any hidden order routing are **unverified**; a licensed exchange is assumed to execute any intent the user takes elsewhere.
- Does any licensed market-data vendor grant worldwide redistribution and derived-valuation rights at a retail price? Coinbase's terms forbid valuations and derived works ([Coinbase](https://www.coinbase.com/legal/market_data)).
