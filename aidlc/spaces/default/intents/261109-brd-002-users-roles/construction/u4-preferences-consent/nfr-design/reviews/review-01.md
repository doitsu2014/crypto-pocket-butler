### Summary

The outbox is the right shape for audit completeness, but its delivery path is under-specified: the drainer-down case has no alert because the alert metrics come from the drainer itself, and the outbox row carries no retry state, so a permanently failing row is re-picked forever. Alongside that, the legacy consent mapping collapses the marketing and analytics purposes into one kind, which misstates recorded consent, and the withdrawal's `FOR UPDATE` does not actually prevent two racing withdrawals from both appending a withdrawal event.
