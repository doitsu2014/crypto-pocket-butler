### Summary

The delivery core is well built: the dedupe insert, the mandatory-email exemption with its wake flag, the 1/5-minute retries and abandonment, the per-delivery C3 read with permanent-versus-transient classification, the content guard with its allow-lists and sanitisation, the twelve-month retention sweep, the single-flight lease and the owner-checked C1 routes are all sound, and the 119 tests pass. Three issues block: the normal five-second delivery path records no delivery metrics, so the mandatory-abandoned page and the delivery SLOs are blind; the in-app preference never reaches the feed, so a type the user turned off in-app is still shown; and the mandatory-email path cannot deliver until u3-identity serves the C3 contact endpoint. The remaining Minor items are the missing route/status metric labels, the email-cap race, run-scoped timestamps, the narrowed audit context, a non-transactional preference save, four traceability targets, the unrun adapter suite, the gateway route wiring and the missing service-token seeding step.
