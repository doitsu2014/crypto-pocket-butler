### Validation performed

Read the four stage artifacts in full, plus the unit's functional-spec §8 and §3.4–§3.6, security-design §14, logical-components §6, performance-requirements, the NFR21–NFR26 rows in the intent requirements, performance-design §9, and the intent contract summary C1/C2 and C3.

Checked against the repository:

- `infra/swarm-stack.yml`: `web` (2 replicas, 0.25 CPU/128M, `read_only` with tmpfs, `/healthz` 30s, `stop_grace_period: 15s`, start-first), `gateway` (2 replicas, 0.5 CPU/512M, `UPSTREAM_TIMEOUT_MS: "2000"`, `GATEWAY_RATE_LIMIT_PER_MINUTE: "120"`, anonymous 30, start-first) and `identity-api` (3 replicas, 0.5 CPU/512M, `IDENTITY_API_POOL: "12"`, `read_only` with tmpfs, `/healthz` only, start-first). Confirmed no Prometheus/Grafana/Loki/Tempo service in the Swarm stack or the local compose stack, and only alert-rule YAML under `infra/prometheus/`.
- `services/gateway`: `domain/src/route.rs` deny-by-default table contains none of the settings, export or telemetry paths and `Route` carries `max_requests_per_minute`; `http/src/rate_limit.rs` keys `check` on forwarded address and `check_route` on route label plus bearer-token SHA-256; `http/src/middleware.rs` runs both buckets; `http/src/metrics.rs` exposes status-class and 5xx counters plus `service_up` only.
- `services/identity`: six claimed routes are absent except D3 (`POST /v1/privacy/deletions`) and the two export routes, which exist; `metrics.rs` renders `http_requests_total{service,status_class,route}`, `identity_request_errors_total`, `identity_deletion_status_total` (requested/in_progress/completed/failed), `identity_principal_status_total`; `middleware.rs` `route_label` is a static match list; `problem.rs` `not_found` renders `https://errors.cpb/identity/not-found`; migrations create `users`, `user_preferences`, `deletion_requests`, `consent_records`.
- `web/`: `scripts/check-size.mjs` exists and measures route chunks; `package.json` size-limit budgets are 300 kB initial, 150 kB `route-*.js`, 150 kB CSS, all gzip; `vite.config.ts` names `*Page` chunks `route-[name]-[hash].js`; `nginx.conf` has no `/v1` proxy, serves `connect-src 'self'`, hashed `/assets/` immutable for a year, `index.html` no-store, internal-only stub_status on 8081; `src/telemetry.ts` posts to `/v1/telemetry/client-errors` by default; the `web` Dockerfile builds both the SPA and the support console into one nginx image.
- `scripts/check-boundaries.sh` and `scripts/smoke-identity.sh` both exist with the behaviour the artifacts describe.
- No CI platform configuration exists anywhere in the repository (no `.github/`, GitLab, Jenkins, CircleCI, Azure Pipelines or Buildkite files).

No stage validation tools were named in the dispatch. No repository-changing command was run.

**Summary.** The no-new-container, no-stack-change core is sound and most of its stack claims check out. The blocking problems are a gateway-entry list that adds an uncontracted ninth path, and a monitoring design that presents route-scoped and latency measurements the repository does not produce.
