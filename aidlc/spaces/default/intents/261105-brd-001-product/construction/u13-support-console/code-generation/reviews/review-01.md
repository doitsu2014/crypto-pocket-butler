### Summary

The console is well built on the core controls: the session is memory-only with an in-memory Supabase adapter and `persistSession: false`, the role gate hides data without a support/admin role, the account search and metadata read require a grant id and the client strips account and ticket fields to the metadata whitelist, the grant expiry fails closed, the grant clock refreshes at expiry, the incident and grant flows are present, and the suite passes 168 tests across 28 files with the build under budget. Two Majors block: the free-text read reason is sent in the URL and will be captured by gateway and nginx access logs (and browser history), breaking the reason-hash/no-PII boundary; and the `/support` path is not actually outside the customer PWA service worker's scope, because not registering a worker for it does not stop the customer's root-scoped worker from controlling `/support` and `/v1`, so the privilege separation the infrastructure design claims is not implemented. The remaining items (joint-delivery and weak traceability targets, the unscoped ticket list with the requester label, the CSP gateway-origin gap, the unwhitelisted grant and incident DTOs, and the missing manual accessibility sign-off) are Minor.
