### Summary

This is a strong, unusually testable artifact: all twelve screens carry ASCII, exact copy and explicit states, the six interview answers are applied consistently, the security-bearing copy holds (identical non-revealing messages for credentials, magic link, verification and recovery; no token, credential or full fingerprint in the login-alert row; no upstream detail in the outage message), and the accessibility checklist is specific enough to check in a pull request. Two Majors should be settled before Development starts: the credential-change and step-up screens that US3.3 and US5.4 need are named but never drawn, and the token mapping certifies the primary-button label at a contrast ratio the tokens do not produce. Neither is structural, so this is READY under the findings rule with the two Majors carried into the gate as Request-Changes material.
