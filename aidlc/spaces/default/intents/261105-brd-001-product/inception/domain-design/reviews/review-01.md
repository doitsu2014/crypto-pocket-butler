### Summary

The catalogue is structurally clean and faithfully follows all six interview answers: one component per service, a separate Ledger, no Billing, Notification owning all delivery, WebApp plus SupportConsole, and a separate Audit. The blocking concerns are gaps in the behaviour the design assigns: transaction ingestion has no workable source or handoff, the privacy operations (export and deletion) cannot run across service boundaries as drawn, the new-device security email has no non-cyclic route to Notification, and the jurisdiction and age gate has no owner.
