### Summary

The design covers the two authentication paths, Vault handling, provider limits and the metric set at a reasonable level, but it leaves three load-bearing areas unresolvable: the lease has no renewal or fencing before the sweep releases the guard, there is no health or readiness probe design behind a health-gated rolling update, and the internal caller-identity mechanism contradicts the single shared service token in the passed contract. The traceability file also reports OK for six rows whose named sections do not exist, so its coverage reading should not be relied on until the targets are corrected.
