### Summary

The design is not implementable as written: the idempotency scheme cannot exist on the partitioned table, the retention service is granted nothing it needs to run, and export downloads land on replicas that do not hold the file. Beyond those, the traceability table claims coverage for five requirements that have no design and omits all eight observability requirements, and several security and reliability controls are asserted without the mechanism that would enforce them.
