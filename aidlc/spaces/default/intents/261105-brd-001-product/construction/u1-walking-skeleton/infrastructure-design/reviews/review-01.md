### Summary

The infrastructure choices match the fixed project rules and the interview answers are recorded honestly with their risks, but four implementation-level gaps remain: the SPA shell has no deployment mapping, the database has no sizing or connection budget, services have no stated path to fetch and rotate their Vault secrets, and the claimed automatic Swarm rollback contradicts Swarm's default behaviour. The monitoring design also needs its SLI definitions and metrics aligned, and the traceability table is missing this unit's observability requirements.
