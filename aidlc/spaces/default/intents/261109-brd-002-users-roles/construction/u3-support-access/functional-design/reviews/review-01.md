## Review

**Verdict:** NOT-READY
**Reviewer:** aidlc-architecture-reviewer-agent
**Iteration:** 1
**Verdict Dictated:** no
**Date:** 2026-11-11T08:05:01Z

The grant lifecycle, the metadata allowlist, the caller binding, the refusal codes and the audit-event set are well specified, and most of the "what already exists" claims check out against the code. Two things do not survive contact with implementation: one lifecycle edge the design says it persists is forbidden by the existing schema, and the audit design has two unresolved mismatches against the C9 contract and the existing `AuditOutcome` type.

### Summary

The design is honest about its cross-unit dependencies and its allowlist is genuinely narrow, but it cannot be implemented as written: the persisted `pending_approval` to `expired` edge violates the existing `support_grants_approval_shape` constraint, and the audit model uses an outcome value and refusal mechanism that neither C9 nor the existing `AuditOutcome` type supports.
