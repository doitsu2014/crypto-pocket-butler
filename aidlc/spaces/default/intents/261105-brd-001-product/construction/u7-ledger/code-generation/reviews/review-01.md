### Summary

The append-only model, the canonical and external dedupe keys, the FIFO lot engine with fee attachment, the commit-first correction audit with retry, the per-field correction validation with the effective old value, the subject-based anonymisation through the narrow anonymiser role, the ownership checks and the 122-test suite are all in place. Four Majors block the P&L: unrealised figures are marked at the latest ledger price or average cost rather than the market and a missing price is silently zero; coverage is computed only from the ledger's own lots, so holdings with no history can be labelled complete; an unmatched external transfer_out leaves the outgoing lots counted; and cost basis is not converted to the user's base currency, so totals can mix currencies. The remaining items (the snapshot/rebuild performance deviations, the deferred OTLP spans and retention sweep, the inbound transfer pair, empty-account discovery, weak traceability targets and standalone fees) are Minor.
