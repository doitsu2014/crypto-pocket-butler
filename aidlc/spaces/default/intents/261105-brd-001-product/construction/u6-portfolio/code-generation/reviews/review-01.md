### Summary

The bulk of the unit is well built: the decimal valuation with provenance, unpriced and C5-unavailable labels, the drifted-point math over the priced total, immutable snapshots with frozen inputs and digest reproduction, the advisory intents with projection and shortfall, the leased run with renewal, the overlay model, the guardrail crossing state, and the owner-scoped routes all follow the design, and the 184 tests pass. One Critical blocks: the privacy erasure renames portfolios but keeps the user id, so the user's data stays linked to the erased identity and NFR13.1 fails. Six Majors follow: the read can serve a pre-trade snapshot inside the 60-second window, the intent preview uses the 800 ms fresh valuation instead of the snapshot's lines, the history read reproduces every snapshot and returns an unbounded window, the run's four-way concurrency misses the 15-minute budget, the overlay reconciliation window can permanently miss a late-synced trade and double count, and the FX path is unreachable because the C6 price carries no currency. The remaining items are Minor: the C7 trade shape and replay dedupe, the erasure audit action, the f64 percentages, the Identity preference fallback, the export's `verified` flag, the guardrail reset on save, the alert payload, the run-level failure classification, the missing prune, and the unrecorded TDD/coverage/integration evidence.
