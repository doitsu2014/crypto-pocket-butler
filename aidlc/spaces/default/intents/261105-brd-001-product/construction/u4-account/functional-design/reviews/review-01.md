### Summary

The design names the right workflows, but several of them cannot be built as written: a spam holding has no valid identifier in the entity model, and CSV ownership, the supported-chain source, the SPA-facing contract, credential replacement and sync recovery each leave a decision that crosses another unit. The credential, notification and privacy obligations are also incomplete against the shared contracts.
