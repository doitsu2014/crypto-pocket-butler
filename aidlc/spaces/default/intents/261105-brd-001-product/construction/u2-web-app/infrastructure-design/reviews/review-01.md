### Summary

Static hosting, the `/support` split, the PWA bypass rules and the rolling rollback are sound, but the design and the shipped `web/nginx.conf` disagree on the headers the SPA and WalletConnect depend on: the CSP omits the WalletConnect origins (blocking C14) and the SPA cache headers are absent. The CI gates also name a Yarn command that Yarn 4 does not provide and omit the Lighthouse and browser-axe measurements the NFRs name, nginx has no metric source for the edge SLI, and NFR9.1 still contradicts the session design; these block READY.
