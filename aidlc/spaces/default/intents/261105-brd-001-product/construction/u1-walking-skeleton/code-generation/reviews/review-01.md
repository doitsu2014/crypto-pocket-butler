### Summary

The skeleton is genuinely built, tested, and boundary-clean, and the runtime behaviour asked about checks out: the gateway validates the JWT before routing, denies by default, honours or generates the correlation id, and forwards the token unchanged; unpriced is a distinct outcome with no zero variant and the price field is absent from the JSON; RFC 7807 bodies carry `correlationId` in both services. The blocking concerns are architectural rather than mechanical - a registry change can land unaudited when the audit sink fails, the gateway exposes three families of routes no shared contract declares, and the rate limiter can be sidestepped by forging `X-Forwarded-For`.
