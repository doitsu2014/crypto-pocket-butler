### Summary

The design is largely implementable and covers every NFR category, but two gaps need resolving before code generation: the worker lease is shorter than the work it protects (so the stated no-double-execution guarantee is false), and the support-read grant check has no assigned enforcement point or contract operation. The remaining findings are small traceability, alert-threshold, capacity-budget and cross-reference corrections.
