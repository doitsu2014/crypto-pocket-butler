### Summary

The mechanical checks all pass, but the model cannot express two things the rules depend on: which asset is a chain's native asset (BR1.1, WF1) and a per-asset price-source priority (Q3), and US1.2's error and empty-state criteria are marked covered without any rule defining their behaviour. The registry design also takes on price resolution and an unreachable registry-administration path, so a developer would have to ask the architect before building the first valuation flow.
