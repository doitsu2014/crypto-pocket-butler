### Summary

The unit's NFR set is quantitatively specific but internally inconsistent at the storage and latency layers: the role matrix forbids the UPDATEs the support-session, retention-run, and export state machines require, and the session-check target is smaller than the audit write the check must perform first. Both are blocking design errors a developer would hit immediately, before the quieter gaps around caller-declared event class, retry ownership, and idempotency expiry.
