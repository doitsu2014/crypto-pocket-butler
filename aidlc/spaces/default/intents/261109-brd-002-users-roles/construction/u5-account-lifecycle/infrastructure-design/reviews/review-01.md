### Summary

The design is well grounded where it borrows from u4's live-safe migration pattern and it does catch a dead sweep, but the rollback path undoes the exact control the unit exists to add - it drops the tombstone while permitting that drop for every erased account - and three of the new data rules (parked `failed` rows, legacy backfill, the unindexed tombstone) leave accounts or queries in states nothing recovers from.
