### Summary

The design is coherent on the grant lifecycle, fail-closed audit, and timeout/retry budgets, but six Major gaps keep it from being implementable as written: the 500 ms read deadline excludes grant evaluation, the per-user rate limit does not exist in the shipped limiter, the metrics type is placed across a forbidden crate boundary, the rollback discards undelivered audit rows, the backlog gauges contradict NFR1.8, and the erasure-retention claim cannot run against the real grants and roles.
