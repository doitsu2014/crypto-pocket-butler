### Summary

The lifecycle rules are well grounded and the traceability is structurally complete, but the design does not survive contact with the schema and the service boundaries: it deletes the very users row its terminal state and denial rule depend on, cannot store disabled or pending_deletion until a migration it never mentions, claims a transactionally atomic audit write to a separate service, and leaves the expiry job's claim, attempt bound and resume semantics undefined. Fix the delete-versus-anonymise contradiction and the pending-status ownership question first; the remaining Majors are then mechanical.
