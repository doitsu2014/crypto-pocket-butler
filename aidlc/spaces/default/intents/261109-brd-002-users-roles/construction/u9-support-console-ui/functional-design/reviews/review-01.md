### Summary

The design is well grounded: every load-bearing claim I checked (the `grantId` versus `id` split, the four statuses, the 60-minute window from approval, the metadata route and its 403 set, the absent by-id and `/support/accounts` routes) matches the shipped code and C5, and the story coverage table is honest that U3 owns the rules. Two things to settle at the gate: `mine` is support-role-only while the shell calls it for admin sessions (a guaranteed 403 plus a full cache clear), and W4 places Identity and gateway work inside a unit defined as UI-only with no owner named.
