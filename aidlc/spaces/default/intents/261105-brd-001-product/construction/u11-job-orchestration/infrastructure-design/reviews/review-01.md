### Summary

The deployment shape is sound (one image, one Swarm service, two replicas, an elected scheduler, port 8105 on the encrypted internal overlay, no public port), but five Major gaps block implementation: the lease TTL is 30 s in one artifact and 60 s in three others, so the promised 90 s failover cannot be delivered; the runtime role can write the service-token registry; the still-failing alert cannot fire for a job that has never succeeded; the release smoke's operator credential is undefined; and CI never runs the required database-backed integration tests.
