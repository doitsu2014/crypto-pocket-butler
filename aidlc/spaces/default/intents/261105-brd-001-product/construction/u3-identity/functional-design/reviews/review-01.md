### Summary

The workflows and rule set are well organised, but several of the unit's Must stories cannot be implemented from the passed contracts: MFA needs an assurance level and a credential fact that no contract supplies, every user is created `pending` with no path to `active`, export and deletion call services the shared contracts do not expose, and the jurisdiction gate has no server-side enforcement point. The deletion and crypto-shredding design also needs a key lifecycle and a Supabase Auth cleanup step before it can meet FR8.2 and NFR13.
