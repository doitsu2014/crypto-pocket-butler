### Summary

The design is well argued at the requirement level, but four things make it unbuildable without an architect's decision: the status-read timeouts do not fit the 500 ms deadline and one of the two status adapters has no path, the designed C4 request contradicts the contract in hand, the role-change workflow is placed in the http layer, and the support rule denies the support role its only operation.
