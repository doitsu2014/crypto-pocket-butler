- 2026-11-10T09:46:24Z — [unit u2-authorisation] the audit write and the role write are not retried. A duplicate audit entry is tolerable, but a duplicate role change or a retry storm against a failing audit store is not, so both surface their failure instead.
- 2026-11-11T08:39:09Z — [unit u3-support-access] a refusal is designed slower than a rule check would be (60/150 ms, not 15/40 ms) because its audit row commits before the response. That is the price of never returning an unrecorded refusal (NFR19.1), and it is still well inside the write budget.
