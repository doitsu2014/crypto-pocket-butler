### Summary

The infrastructure is mostly the right shape for a unit that adds no deployable, but its compliance-critical control does not work as written: the erasure role cannot be granted or assumed as the table describes, and the audit-durability monitoring it relies on may never be collected or may never fire. The migration and traceability artifacts also claim more than they state, so the unit is not ready to build until the role mechanism, the drainer's host, the migration's transaction and lock plan, and the NFR coverage are settled.
