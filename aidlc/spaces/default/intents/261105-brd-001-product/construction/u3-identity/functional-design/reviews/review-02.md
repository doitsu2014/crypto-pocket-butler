> R-01 Reviewer note: Identity now reads the `aal` and `app_metadata.has_exchange_credentials` claims from the JWT it validates itself, and WF1 keeps the user `pending` until both are satisfied, so no Account lookup or C3 field is needed for the facts. The unowned producer of the claim is a new finding.

> R-02 Reviewer note: BR1.5 now defines the transition, but the input is written as "Supabase's confirmed/email_confirmed claim". That is two possible names, neither defined in this design or in C3, so an implementer still cannot tell what proves a verified email or what happens when confirmation is disabled.

> R-03 Reviewer note: The six export and anonymise operations are now specified as change requests with owning units, and the private `identity-exports` bucket names the store, reader and deletion window. The shared contract summary and component dependency table still do not carry the operations, and Identity is still not a consumer of C5, C7 or C8, so Code Generation still cannot build the orchestration.

> R-04 Reviewer note: WF6 step 5 now has Supabase Auth's pre-sign-up hook verify a short-lived signed gate token, which gives the gate a server-side enforcement point outside the client. The recorded Q7 answer mismatch is a new finding.

> R-05 Reviewer note: WF1 step 3 creates the ShreddingKey reference, the entity scopes the key to Identity's own personal fields, excludes other services' legal-retention records, and BR6.3 makes destruction the last and testable completion step.

> R-06 Reviewer note: The approve operation and `grantId` are now change requests, but C2 still lacks `revoked` and the approver fields, requires `expiresAt` at creation while the design sets it only on approval, and WF7 step 4 still leaves open whether Account or Identity checks the grant on the metadata read.

> R-07 Reviewer note: WF9 step 4 now deletes the Supabase Auth user and its factors, states that sign-in is blocked, anonymises consent history, and makes audit actor references pseudonymous. The BR6.4 citation is a new finding.

> R-08 Reviewer note: DeviceSession, its coarse-location source and retention rule, the reconciliation against Auth and the provider-failure fallback are now defined. The gateway transport for device and location is a new finding.
