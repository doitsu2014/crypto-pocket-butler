### Summary

The four surfaces, their states, the Parchment Ledger mapping, the accessibility structure, and the four interaction flows are substantially present and usable. The blocking issue is the deletion and disabled-account experience: the mockups give a denied account a banner on screens the requirements say it cannot reach and never define the gate it actually lands on. Two requirement conflicts in the support metadata boundary and the role-badge fallback should also be settled before engineering starts.
