### Summary

The append-only consent model is a sound choice and the workflows read cleanly, but the design's centrepiece — the first-source consent gate — has no callable interface in C6, its scope contradicts BR4.7, and a new consumer appears in the spec that no unit or contract owns. The preference defaults also cannot coexist with the "never overwritten" rule as written. Fix the gate interface and the contract, and reconcile the remaining rules, before this goes to code generation.
