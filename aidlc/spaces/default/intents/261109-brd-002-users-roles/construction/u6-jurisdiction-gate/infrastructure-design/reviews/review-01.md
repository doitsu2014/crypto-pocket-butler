### Summary

The design correctly targets the real platform — Docker Swarm, self-hosted Supabase, no AWS — and is honest about the missing CI platform, but it names a migration number that does not fit the Identity service, a publish role that is not provisioned, and marks the client-IP trust requirement covered without specifying how the edge and gateway produce a trusted address. Fix those three plus the readiness contract and the incomplete NFR traceability before building.
