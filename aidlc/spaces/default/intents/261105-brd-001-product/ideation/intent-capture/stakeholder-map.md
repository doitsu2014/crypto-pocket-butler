## Decision-Makers vs Influencers

- The product owner decides scope and priority. [Q3]
- Engineering, security, and legal leads influence those decisions. [Q3]
- Customers are not named as decision-makers or influencers in the confirmed governance answer. [Q3]

## Communication Requirements

- Written status is reported at each workflow stage gate. [Q3]
- Decisions are recorded in the repository. [Q3]
