### Summary

The catalogue is structurally sound and answers every boundary question the interview asked, but implementation is blocked by unowned Must requirements: role changes and their audit record (FR20.1, FR20.2) have no component or data path, and pending-deletion denial, continued-use jurisdiction blocking, entry-gate acceptances, and the support metadata projection all lack an owning component or a data source. The Authorisation interaction style across services is also left open in ADR-002, which determines whether every service gains a synchronous dependency on Identity.
