### Summary

The unit's targets are well measured and its failure matrix is reasoned, but the design rests on three things the stack does not currently give it: an append-only consent table that cannot survive a failed audit write, an existing `consent_records` and `users` schema the migration plan does not acknowledge, and a C6 contract that does not contain the gate endpoint the whole design is built around. The Critical finding is the audit ordering on withdrawals, which makes a 100% audit-completeness promise unachievable by construction.
