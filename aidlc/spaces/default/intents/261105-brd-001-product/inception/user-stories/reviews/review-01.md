The stories are well formed and largely testable: 37 stories all in the persona/want/so-that form with 3–4 Given/When/Then criteria, grouped by user journey, and the interview decisions (free and open source, Binance and OKX, three chains, production only) hold with no residual paid-plan or staging language.
