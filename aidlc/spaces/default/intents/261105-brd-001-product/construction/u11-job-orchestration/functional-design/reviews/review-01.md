### Summary

The core scheduling model is not implementable as written: the run-record retry rules contradict the immutability rule, and the synchronous tick loop lets one long job starve the heartbeat and every other window, so the unit's own alerting and missed-run guarantees fail. Ten further cross-reference and completeness gaps against C11, C9, FR10.1 and components.md require revision before the design can be built without guessing.
