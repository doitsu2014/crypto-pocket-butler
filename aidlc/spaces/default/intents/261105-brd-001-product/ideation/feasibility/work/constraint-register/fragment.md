## Notes for synthesis

- **Launch-blocking:** CON-025 (market-data redistribution and derived-valuation rights) is the
  single constraint that can stop the product without any engineering failure. It should be
  carried into the RAID log as a high-severity risk with a named owner and a pre-launch gate.
- **Tension to record:** CON-013 (5-minute freshness) and CON-025 (24-hour cache ceiling and
  attribution) are compatible in principle but only if polling cadence and display rights are
  confirmed per vendor; treat the combined position as unresolved.
- **Second-order cost:** CON-006 (no cross-schema grants) plus CON-008 (no message queues) push
  composition and deferred work into HTTP calls and Postgres-backed job records. This is an
  accepted architectural cost, not a defect, but it raises the value of the Asset Registry and
  Job Orchestration services.
- **Not constraints:** presentation-layer choices, observability vendor selection and
  localisation scope are open design decisions and are deliberately excluded from this register.
