### Summary

The infrastructure shape is small and mostly honest about what it does not add, but it places the new outbox drainer in a service that does not run one, and its rollback path cannot empty the outbox that its own down migration refuses to drop without emptying. With the metadata hop's account URL unresolvable as shipped, a third of the unit's requirement ids untraced, and every alert dependent on collectors that exist nowhere in the repository, whoever builds this would be guessing at the points that matter most.
